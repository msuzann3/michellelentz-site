About

I’ve built my career helping people make sense of change.

My work has taken me through technical writing, learning design, facilitation, coaching, and AI enablement. Across those roles, I keep coming back to the same questions: What do people need to understand? What do they need to do? And what will help them get there?

My story

Helping people understand what comes next.

I started in technical communication, translating complicated systems and ideas into information people could use. Learning design expanded that work: helping people build skills, practice decisions, and apply what they learned.

With 15+ years in learning and facilitation, I’ve worked across classrooms, virtual training, webinars, and conferences. Coaching and change management added another perspective: understanding how people grow, what gets in their way, and what support makes a difference.

Today, I bring those experiences to AI strategy and enablement. I connect decisions about technology with learning, governance, and the work people actually need to do.

I’m also co-founder and director of L&D Cares, supporting coaching and mentoring in the HR and Talent community.
