Self-directed portfolio sample · Adaptive eLearning

Mission: Responsible AI

Three AI systems are creating risk. Learners investigate the evidence, install safeguards, and contain the harm in an adaptive field lab based on my From Risk to Responsibility white paper.

I created this self-directed sample for my portfolio to demonstrate my current approach to scenario-based eLearning and responsible AI.

I designed the concept, subject-matter approach, learning objectives, mission structure, scenarios, and decision points, refining the experience through repeated review and iteration. I used ChatGPT Codex to help build and improve the working web experience.

Learning design

Read. Act. Watch it adapt.

Learners examine mission evidence, make decisions, request intelligence when needed, and monitor a secure channel for changing support and consequences. Each mission responds independently to what the learner notices, which safeguards they install, and what they do next.

LMS delivery

Ready for an LMS-friendly wrapper

The current course is a standalone web experience, but its interaction model can be instrumented for LMS delivery. A SCORM version could report bookmarking, completion, and score.

An xAPI or cmi5 implementation could additionally capture mission choices, safeguard decisions, adaptive routes, and incident responses in a Learning Record Store.
