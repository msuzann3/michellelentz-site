AI Organizational Readiness Tool

For Innovate Elevate, I needed a useful starting point for conversations with potential clients: a way to understand their AI maturity before recommending where to begin.

I built a self-assessment grounded in established AI maturity research. As the tool evolved, it became both a practical readiness diagnostic and an interactive call to action that can open the door to a more focused conversation.

Why I built it

I needed a consistent, credible way to begin AI strategy conversations with potential clients—not with a sales pitch or a tool recommendation, but with a clearer picture of their current maturity.

Research foundation

I reviewed established AI maturity studies and translated recurring themes into practical questions spanning workforce, governance, data, technology, and operations.

What I produced

A live self-assessment that helps organizations see patterns across readiness dimensions, identify potential gaps, and establish a more grounded starting point for planning.

How it evolved

The diagnostic also became an interactive lead-generation experience. Visitors receive an immediate readiness perspective and can choose to share their results for a more focused follow-up conversation.
