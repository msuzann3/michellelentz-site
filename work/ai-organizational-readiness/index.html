Problem

Organizations often pursue AI initiatives before assessing workforce, governance, data, technology, and operational readiness.

My role

I designed the research-informed assessment model and translated it into an interactive experience that organizations and teams can use to evaluate their readiness for agentic-AI adoption.

What I produced

A live self-assessment that evaluates key readiness dimensions, surfaces potential gaps, and gives organizations a more grounded starting point for AI planning.

Evidence/result

Visitors can complete the working assessment and receive an immediate readiness perspective. When they choose to share their results, those results are shared with me for follow-up and a conversation about their readiness needs.
